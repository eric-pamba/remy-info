A private personal assistant

Remy Google Workspace

Remy helps its operator find information, understand commitments and carry out authorized tasks.

Remy runs through Hermes on its operator’s computer. Its Google Workspace connection can work with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, and read Contacts. Access is granted through Google’s consent screen for each connected account.

The connection supports tasks such as summarizing email, reviewing calendars and working with documents. Sending messages, changing events or editing files requires applicable user authorization.

Relevant Google information may be processed by external AI services and retained in local conversation history or memory. The privacy notice explains this data flow and how to revoke access.
